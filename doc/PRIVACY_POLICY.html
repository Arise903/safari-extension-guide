本应用所有拦截、统计与诊断处理均在您的设备本地完成，不收集、不上传、不共享任何个人数据。应用内无账号系统、无广告 SDK、无数据分析 SDK、无崩溃上报服务。

一、概述

本应用是一款在您设备本地运行的广告拦截工具。它通过 Android VpnService 在 DNS 层拦截广告、追踪器和恶意域名。所有拦截、统计与诊断处理均在您的设备本地完成。

我们秉持「数据最小化」原则：本应用不收集、不上传、不共享任何个人数据或设备数据到任何外部服务器。

二、我们处理的数据

数据类型用途存储位置是否离开设备

三、数据安全

所有拦截规则、统计与设置均存储于应用私有沙盒目录，其他应用无法访问。我们不运营任何接收或存储您数据的服务器。本地数据在设备存储中以标准方式保存；DNS 查询仅转发至您选择的上游 DNS（Cloudflare / Google / AdGuard 等），查询内容不会与开发者共享。

四、数据保留

由于我们不向服务器传输任何个人数据，不存在服务器端的个人数据保留。设备本地的统计与日志会随您清除应用数据或卸载应用而删除。

All filtering, statistics, and diagnostics are processed locally on your device. This app does not collect, upload, or share any personal data. There is no account system, no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, and no crash-reporting service.

1. Overview

This app is an on-device ad blocker. It uses the Android VpnService to block ads, trackers, and malicious domains at the DNS layer. All filtering, statistics, and diagnostics are processed locally on your device.

We follow a data-minimization principle: this app does not collect, upload, or share any personal or device data to any external server.

2. Data We Process

2.2 Data we do NOT collect

Names, emails, phone numbers; precise or approximate location; device identifiers (IMEI, Android ID, Advertising ID); contacts, SMS, call logs; microphone, camera, photos, file contents; cross-app behavioral profiles; any data for advertising or marketing.

3. Data Security

All rules, stats, and settings are stored in the app's private sandbox, inaccessible to other apps. We operate no servers that receive or store your data. Local data is kept in standard on-device storage. DNS queries are forwarded only to your chosen upstream resolver (Cloudflare / Google / AdGuard); query contents are never shared with the developer.

4. Data Retention

Because we transmit no personal data to any server, there is no server-side personal data retention. On-device stats and logs are removed when you clear app data or uninstall the app.

6. Third-Party Services

This app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs. To fetch public filter rules, the app downloads rule files over standard HTTPS from providers (e.g., AdGuard public filter lists); only rule text is transferred, never your personal data or DNS queries. The built-in browser uses the system WebView, subject to your device browser's own policy.

7. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from any age group.

8. Your Rights

As we collect and upload no personal data, there is no server data for us to delete or export. You may clear all local data at any time by uninstalling the app.

9. Policy Changes

If data handling changes in the future, we will update the effective date and notify users.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent via the in-app feedback channel or the project repository Issues.
